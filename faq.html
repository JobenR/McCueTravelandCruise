Frequently asked

Questions, answered

Straight answers about planning fees, timing, cabins, documents and more.

Do you charge a fee to plan my cruise?

Standard cruise bookings often carry no planning fee because cruise lines compensate travel advisors directly. Multi-part, group, wedding and fully custom itineraries may carry a planning fee, always quoted upfront. See our pricing page.

Will I pay more than if I booked directly with the cruise line?

Cruise lines generally price consistently across channels, so you typically won’t pay more by booking with an advisor — and you gain personal guidance, support and often added perks.

How far in advance should I book?

For ocean cruises, 6 – 12 months ahead gives the best choice of cabins and prices. River cruises, holiday sailings and expeditions often sell out 12 – 24 months ahead. Last-minute deals do exist — ask us.

What’s the difference between interior, oceanview, balcony and suite cabins?

Interior cabins have no window and offer the best value. Oceanview cabins have a window. Balcony cabins add private outdoor space. Suites add extra room, perks and priority services.

Is travel insurance necessary?

We strongly recommend it. Coverage can protect against trip cancellation, medical emergencies, delays and lost baggage. We’ll explain options and what each does and doesn’t cover.

Do I need a passport?

For most international cruises, yes. Requirements depend on your citizenship, ports of call and departure port. We provide document guidance for every trip; always confirm with official government sources.

What happens if my plans change?

Policies vary by supplier and timing. Contact us as early as possible and we’ll review your options, handle changes and help with claims where applicable.

Can you book flights and hotels too?

Yes. We coordinate air, transfers and pre- or post-cruise stays so everything lines up.

Can you help with special dietary or accessibility needs?

Absolutely. Let us know your needs at the start. We’ll select ships, cabins and excursions that accommodate them and notify the cruise line for you.

Do you plan trips for solo travelers?

Yes. Some lines offer solo cabins and reduced single supplements, and we’ll recommend sailings that are friendly to solo guests.

How do group bookings work?

We reserve a block of cabins, give you a personalized booking link to share, track each household’s payments and coordinate details. Perks vary by sailing. See Groups & Corporate.

Can you plan a destination wedding?

Yes: ceremonies, guest travel, room blocks and honeymoons. Start 12 – 18 months out for the widest selection, though shorter timelines can work. See Weddings & Honeymoons.

How do I get started?

Fill out the contact form or call us. We’ll schedule a no-obligation conversation, learn what you’re dreaming of and send tailored options.

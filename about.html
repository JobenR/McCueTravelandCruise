Our story

Built on the belief that great trips are planned by people who care

McCue Travel & Cruise began with a simple idea: planning a vacation should feel as good as taking one. Behind every booking is a real advisor who listens first, recommends honestly, and stays involved long after the deposit is paid.

We specialize in cruising — ocean, river and expedition — and the celebrations that go with it: weddings, honeymoons, reunions and milestone trips. We’ve learned that the details travelers remember most are the ones that quietly went right.

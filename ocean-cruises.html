Choosing a cabin

Where you sleep shapes your trip

The difference between a good cruise and a great one is often the cabin. We’ll explain each category and steer you toward — or away from — specific rooms.

  • Interior: the best value. Ideal if you’re rarely in the room.
  • Oceanview: a window for natural light and sunrise views.
  • Balcony: private outdoor space; our most-requested upgrade.
  • Suite: extra space, priority services and dedicated lounges.

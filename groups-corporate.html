Why book as a group

Perks that add up

Group bookings can unlock added value. Specific perks vary by cruise line and sailing, and we’ll tell you exactly what’s available.

  • Group amenities such as onboard credit or cabin upgrades, where offered
  • Complimentary berths for group leaders on qualifying bookings, where offered
  • Flexible deposit and payment schedules for each household
  • Private events, group dining and shared excursion options
  • Dedicated group coordinator and booking link

How group planning works

  1. Share your goals: group size, budget and wish list.
  2. Receive options: two or three itineraries with pricing and perks.
  3. Hold the space: a modest group deposit reserves cabins.
  4. Invite your guests: everyone books via your personalized link.
  5. Sail together: we track payments, documents and changes until departure.

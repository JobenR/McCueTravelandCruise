River cruising

Wake up in a new storybook town

River ships dock right in town centers, so you step off and into the story: cobblestone squares, castles and cafés.

  • Smaller ships with 100–200 guests and a relaxed, social feel
  • Guided tours often included in the fare
  • Rhine, Danube, Main, Seine, Douro, Rhône and more
  • Spring tulips, summer sunshine, festive Christmas markets

Expedition voyages

Go where the big ships can’t

Small expedition vessels reach remote coastlines with naturalists, kayaks and Zodiac landings.

  • Galápagos: close wildlife encounters on volcanic islands
  • Antarctica: icebergs, penguins and polar landscapes
  • Arctic & Svalbard: polar bears and midnight sun
  • Remote Pacific & Africa coasts: off-the-beaten-path adventures

Expedition departures are limited and book far in advance — often 12 to 24 months — so early planning pays off.

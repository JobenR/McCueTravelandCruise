Good to know

How payments typically work

Deposits

A deposit holds your cabin at booking. Amounts vary by cruise line and cabin type and are confirmed in writing.

Final payment

Balances are typically due 60 – 120 days before sailing; we send reminders well in advance.

Cancellation & protection

Policies differ by supplier. We walk you through them and discuss optional travel insurance.
